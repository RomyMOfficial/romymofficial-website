1. Standard AI, access and data-handling policy

Our standard operating policy describes our use of AI, human access, APIs, automations, connectors, document uploads, recording and transcription, including purposes, provider categories, security and retention arrangements. The policy will be made available before engagement and incorporated into the service agreement by an identified version. Acceptance of the agreement covers the described methods where reasonably necessary for the agreed work and within the access granted by the client; separate approval is not required for every system or routine operation covered by that policy.

Clients must raise restrictions before work starts. Any agreed exception will be recorded in the engagement schedule. We will respect the exception and explain any consequence for scope, delivery or fees before proceeding. Restrictions raised during an engagement will be assessed before further affected processing, with any revised delivery arrangements agreed in writing.

An exclusion of API access prohibits that method. An exclusion of AI processing also prohibits manually sending the same information to an AI service. These are distinct restrictions. Acceptance of standard terms does not authorise access beyond the agreed work, confer rights the client cannot grant, or replace separately required legal notices or consent.

A proposed use materially outside the accepted policy or agreed scope requires a separate agreement before use. Ordinary tool use within the disclosed policy does not require repeated permission requests.

We ask clients to agree to our terms and this policy, and acknowledge the Privacy Policy, before engagement. Clients can raise restrictions before accepting. Any separately required consent remains specific to the relevant processing.

2. Confidentiality and security

We will use client information only for the agreed services and associated lawful obligations. Client passwords will be stored in a secure 1Password vault and shared through 1Password. API keys and other access credentials needed for local work will be held in local secrets files and removed when the engagement is completed. Local secrets files are access-restricted and excluded from routine Time Machine backups and workspace source control. These exclusions do not mean that every historical or provider-held copy has been erased. For client n8n cloud implementations, operational credentials remain configured in the client-accessible n8n environment; deleting our local copies does not remove credentials needed for the client’s live workflows. Credentials will not be included in deliverable documents or unsecured handover messages. Access will be limited to what is reasonably needed for the work. The tool categories and providers used are described below, with engagement-specific information supplied where relevant. Personnel with access must be subject to appropriate confidentiality arrangements.

We limit access to what the work requires, separate client workspaces, and use the security controls available and configured for the relevant accounts. Our local work computer uses FileVault disk encryption. We do not represent that every provider uses the same security or retention controls. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Identifiable client information must not be used to train a general-purpose model unless that use has been separately and expressly agreed and is lawful. Before identifiable information is processed by an AI provider, the applicable account terms, model-training settings, retention and access arrangements must be checked against the agreed engagement restrictions. Information must not be sent to a product whose settings cannot meet the agreed restrictions.

We will revoke or rotate affected credentials within our control, or coordinate this with the client or provider where required. We will notify you promptly after becoming aware of an incident affecting your information and cooperate with investigation, containment and any legally required notifications. Any specific notification deadline is stated in the engagement schedule; otherwise notice will be given promptly.

3. Handover and deletion

At the agreed close of the engagement, we will package all client-owned assets and agreed deliverables, relevant data exports, instructions, documentation and access details identified in the handover schedule and transfer them through an agreed secure method. The package will identify implemented systems, account ownership, documentation, exports and any known outstanding actions. Credentials will be transferred or managed through a secure access process, not included in the package as plaintext.

Following handover and the agreed support period, we will remove our access where it is no longer needed, disconnect our client-specific integrations and remove client credentials from our password-management tools. We will delete unnecessary working copies of client data within 30 days after the support period ends, including copies in local workspaces, uploads, conversations and other provider systems where deletion is available and within our control.

The deletion record will identify any information that must be retained for legal, financial or dispute purposes, the reason and retention period. Such information will be restricted to that purpose. Copies in provider backups or logs that cannot be immediately deleted will be identified with the applicable expiry or retention arrangements. We will not claim that every provider-held copy has been erased without verifying it.

Client-controlled systems and records remain with the client; deleting our working copies does not mean deleting the client's live system. Reusable RomyM Materials may be retained only after client information and proprietary material have been removed. Handover and offboarding responsibilities are not waived solely because a payment dispute exists.

4. Tools, purposes and minimisation

Depending on the engagement, tools may include OpenAI ChatGPT/Codex and Anthropic Claude for analysis, drafting, development and connected-system work; Fireflies for agreed transcription and meeting summaries; Google Workspace and Notion for documents and project records; Airtable for operational databases; n8n for integrations and workflows; and 1Password for credential management. Client-owned tools may also be used. Listing a tool does not mean all client information is sent to it.

We use only information reasonably needed for the task, remove identifying details where practical, and review AI-assisted advice and deliverables. Connectors, APIs and manual uploads are covered methods only within the agreed scope and granted access. An AI restriction applies to manual uploads as well as connectors. A materially new purpose or provider arrangement outside the disclosed policy must be addressed before processing.

We do not record or transcribe a meeting unless participants have been informed and any required agreement has been obtained. Sensitive information and automated decisions significantly affecting people require a separate assessment of purpose, safeguards and applicable rights before use. Acceptance by a business does not supply consent on behalf of every person in its records.

5. Overseas processing and provider retention

Cloud providers and remote working can involve processing outside Australia. Relevant locations depend on the provider product, hosting, settings and remote-access location. We provide available verified information about relevant locations for an engagement; we do not infer processing countries from a provider's headquarters. Provider retention, security logs and backups can differ from our own deletion timetable. We explain any material limits relevant to the engagement and do not promise immediate erasure of every provider copy.
