1. About this policy

Romy Mueller trading as RomyM Official, ABN 73 514 053 455, is based in Victoria, Australia (we, us, our). We provide growth operations consulting, AI integration and automation services, programs, digital resources and education. This policy explains how we collect, hold, use and disclose personal information when you visit romymofficial.com, join our waitlist or email list, enquire about our services, work with us or purchase our products.

Personal information is information or an opinion about an identified person, or a person who can reasonably be identified. Business information can also be personal information where it identifies someone, such as a founder, employee or customer.

We handle personal information in accordance with the privacy laws that apply to our business. This policy does not replace any additional privacy notice or data handling agreement provided for a particular service.

Privacy contact: hello@romymofficial.com.

2. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on your interaction with us. It may include:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, business name, role and contact details.
  • Information you provide in enquiries, applications, booking forms, waitlist forms, surveys, emails and social media messages.
  • Purchase, billing, payment status and account access records. Payment providers process payment credentials through their own systems; we receive the transaction information needed to manage purchases and invoices.
  • Information about your business, goals and operations that you share during consulting or program delivery, including documents, workflows, reports and correspondence containing personal information.
  • Information about your staff, contractors, clients or customers where access is necessary for agreed work, such as building a CRM, analysing delivery processes or configuring an automation.
  • Meeting recordings, transcripts, notes and action items where recording or transcription has been disclosed and agreed.
  • Testimonials, reviews and feedback you choose to provide, including your name, role, photograph or video where you agree to publication.
  • Technical information such as your IP address, browser and device details, pages requested, referral source and interactions with forms, emails or embedded services.

Our services are intended for adults. We do not seek information about children or sensitive information such as health details, identity documents, religious beliefs or biometric information for ordinary website enquiries or marketing. Please do not include this information unless we have specifically agreed that it is needed for a service. Where sensitive information is necessary, we will explain the purpose and seek consent where required by law.

3. How we collect information

We usually collect information directly from you when you complete a form, book a call, subscribe, purchase, send a message or participate in our services. We may also receive it from an authorised representative, a referral contact or a client who gives us access to their business systems for an agreed project.

Technical information may be collected automatically by our hosting provider and by services you interact with, such as booking, checkout, email and embedded testimonial tools.

You can make a general enquiry without identifying yourself, or use a pseudonym, where practical. We may need accurate contact, billing or business information to provide a service, manage an account or meet legal obligations. If you do not provide information we reasonably need, we may be unable to fulfil your request.

If you provide information about another person, you must have authority to share it and arrange any notice or consent required for the intended use. Please share only what is needed for the work.

4. How we use information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries; assess whether our services are suitable; manage bookings, waitlists, enrolments and purchases; deliver consulting, implementation, programs and resources; provide support; communicate about your engagement; maintain business and financial records; and meet legal obligations.

In consulting and implementation work, this may include reviewing business processes, analysing information, preparing recommendations and configuring systems or automations within the agreed scope.

We may also use information to protect our systems, investigate misuse, resolve complaints and improve our services. We use contact details for marketing where we have consent or another lawful basis to do so. We do not treat a general enquiry or a consulting client's customer database as automatic permission to add people to our marketing list.

We will seek permission before publishing an identifiable testimonial or case study. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

5. Email, waitlists and social messaging

If you subscribe to our email list or waitlist, we use your details to send the updates and marketing you signed up for, including program information and relevant resources. Email services may record delivery, opens and link clicks to help us manage communications and understand engagement.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in the email or by contacting us. Unsubscribing does not stop necessary messages about a purchase, active engagement, account or privacy request. We may retain a limited suppression record to respect your choice.

If you interact with an automated social messaging flow, the platform and messaging provider may process your profile details, messages and responses. An automated messaging flow is not necessarily an AI system.

6. Client systems and information handled on a client's behalf

Some services involve access to personal information held in a client's systems. We handle that information for the agreed work and in accordance with the client's lawful instructions, applicable law and any relevant agreement. We do not use a client's customer or staff information for our own marketing.

The client remains responsible for its own privacy notices, lawful collection and decisions about its business systems. If your information belongs to a client's records, please contact that business first about access, correction or deletion. If you contact us, we will help identify the appropriate contact and assist with requests within our role.

7. Service providers and other disclosures

We use external providers for website hosting, forms and bookings, email, social messaging, payments, course delivery, document storage, project management, databases, automation, meeting transcription and AI-assisted work.

Depending on the interaction or service, these may include Netlify, Kit, TidyCal, ManyChat, ThriveCart, Stripe, PayPal, Google Workspace, Notion, Airtable, n8n, Fireflies and Feedspace. AI providers are described in section 9. A particular engagement may use different providers, including systems selected and owned by the client.

We share information needed for the relevant function. Providers handle information under their applicable terms, privacy policies and contractual arrangements. Some also process information for their own legal, security or payment obligations.

Information may also be disclosed to contractors assisting with agreed work, professional advisers, a person you authorise, or authorities where required or permitted by law. If a proposed sale or restructure of the business requires access to personal information, we will limit disclosure to what is necessary and use appropriate confidentiality protections.

8. Overseas storage and access

Our business uses cloud services, and personal information may be stored, processed or accessed outside Australia. This includes information handled by service providers, AI tools and people working remotely. The location depends on the provider, account settings and engagement.

Before disclosure, we consider the provider's data handling arrangements and the protections appropriate to the information and purpose. Where applicable law requires safeguards for overseas disclosure or transfers, we take the steps required by that law. A provider's privacy policy does not remove our own obligations.

You can contact us to ask which providers and overseas locations are relevant to your engagement.

9. Artificial intelligence and automation

We use AI tools to assist with research, content preparation, drafting communications, analysing business information, developing recommendations, preparing systems documentation, and building or troubleshooting workflows and software. AI may also assist with meeting transcription, summaries and action items.

AI providers may include OpenAI and Anthropic, as well as AI features within other business software. The tools used depend on the task and engagement. These tools may process prompts, uploaded documents, connected records, transcripts and generated outputs that contain personal information.

We limit information shared with AI tools to what is reasonably needed for the task and remove or de-identify personal details where practical. Before using identifiable or sensitive information, we assess the purpose, permissions, provider terms and available privacy settings. Where consent is required, we obtain it separately; publishing this policy is not a substitute for consent.

An AI provider may retain inputs and outputs and process them overseas under the terms and settings applicable to the product and account. We do not authorise identifiable client information to be used to train a general-purpose model unless separately agreed and lawful. We select products and account settings to support that restriction; provider retention for delivery, security or legal purposes may still apply. Please contact us about the arrangements for your engagement before sharing confidential or sensitive material.

We review AI-assisted advice and client deliverables before relying on them or sharing them with you. AI supports our work; responsibility for our recommendations remains with us. Routine automations may carry out agreed tasks, such as sending messages or updating records, without a person reviewing each execution.

If an engagement includes an AI tool that interacts directly with users or supports decisions about individuals, we will address its purpose, data use and required notices in the scope and implementation arrangements for that tool.

You can raise concerns or request limits on the use of your information with AI by emailing us. We will explain available alternatives and any effect on scope, cost or delivery before agreeing how to proceed. Withdrawal of consent applies to future processing based on that consent and does not automatically undo earlier lawful processing or override legal recordkeeping requirements.

10. Meeting recordings and transcription

Where we propose to record or transcribe a meeting, we will tell participants and seek agreement before doing so. Recordings, transcripts and summaries may be processed by an external transcription or AI provider and used to document the discussion and manage the work.

If you prefer not to be recorded or transcribed, tell us before or at the start of the meeting so we can discuss an alternative. We will seek separate permission before using a recording publicly or for a purpose outside the engagement.

11. Cookies and embedded services

Our website and connected services may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies for functions such as forms, preferences, login and checkout. Embedded content and external services may receive technical information when your browser connects to them, including your IP address and the page requested.

The technologies used depend on the page and service you access. You can manage cookies through your browser and use any preference controls provided by the relevant service. Blocking cookies or embedded services may affect some features. Where consent is required for a tracking technology, we will request it before that technology is used.

Links to external websites take you to services with their own privacy practices. Please read their policies before providing personal information.

12. Storage, security and retention

We hold information in business systems, electronic documents, communications and, where relevant, client-managed platforms. We take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Measures depend on the system and may include account access restrictions, secure authentication and provider security controls. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, including delivery, support, financial records, legal obligations and resolving disputes. Retention periods differ between enquiries, subscriber records, client work, recordings and financial records.

When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete it or de-identify it, subject to applicable law and the operation of backups and provider systems. Information in a client's systems is managed under that client's arrangements. If a data breach occurs, we will assess it and notify affected people and authorities where required by law.

13. Access, correction and other requests

You can ask for access to personal information we hold about you, request a correction, ask about its use or request deletion by emailing hello@romymofficial.com. We may need to verify your identity using information proportionate to the request.

We will respond within a reasonable time and meet any applicable statutory deadline. If we cannot fulfil a request, we will explain the reason, subject to legal restrictions, and available complaint options. We do not charge for making a request. If a reasonable access charge is permitted and necessary, we will explain it before proceeding.

Deletion is subject to legal obligations, legitimate recordkeeping needs and other applicable exceptions. Where information is held on a client's behalf, we may need to refer the request to that client.

Depending on the law that applies to your circumstances, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, receive a portable copy of information or complain to your local regulator. We will assess requests under the law that applies; this policy does not assume every privacy law applies to every interaction.

14. Privacy complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your information, email hello@romymofficial.com with the details and the outcome you are seeking. We will acknowledge the complaint, investigate it and aim to provide a substantive response within 30 days. If more time is needed, we will explain why and provide an update.

If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au where the matter falls within its jurisdiction, or another relevant privacy regulator.

15. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our services, tools or legal obligations change. The current published version will show its last updated date. Where a change requires a separate notice or consent, we will provide that notice or seek that consent.

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, contact RomyM Official at hello@romymofficial.com.
