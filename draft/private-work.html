Private Strategy & Implementation

For established coaching and education businesses that want experienced strategic input and someone who can do the work.

Maybe you're considering a major platform migration, want to introduce AI and automation or know there are parts of your business that could be working a lot better.

You might already have a project in mind. Or you might know what you'd like to achieve but aren't sure of the best way to get there.

What we could work on

Every business is different, and so is the work I do.

01

Technology & platform migrations

Outgrown your current setup? I'll help you decide what to keep, what to replace and how everything should work together. From choosing the right platforms to managing the migration and connecting the systems.

02

AI & automation

Finding practical ways to take repetitive work off your team's plate, connect information and introduce AI where it genuinely makes things easier.

03

Business operations & client experience

Improving how enquiries, customers, delivery and internal work move through the business. That might mean redesigning a process, creating better systems or removing steps that no longer serve a purpose.

How we'll work together

You can tell me what you want built. I'll also tell you what I think.

I don't assume the first solution is necessarily the right one.

We'll start by looking at what you're trying to achieve, what's happening in the business and what needs to change.

I'll recommend an approach, taking into account the time, cost and practical implications of making it happen.

Then I can take care of the implementation, from setting up the technology and connecting systems to testing that everything works as intended.

Projects are scoped around what your business needs. You're working directly with me, and you'll know what's happening along the way.
