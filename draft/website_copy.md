## Case Study — Socially Sands / WACA

# Rebuilding the systems behind a growing coaching business

An established coaching and education business with multiple offers, a large membership and regular launches had a growing customer base, but had outgrown its systems. Too much work was manual, and the reporting was difficult to trust.

I migrated the core platforms, automated recurring work, fixed the tracking and improved the checkout setup.

## 1. Platforms & team operations

**Before:** Sales, email marketing and course systems were scattered across overlapping platforms that the business had outgrown. Customer onboarding, data transfers and team task management involved manual double-handling. Information was spread across tools, so the team regularly relied on the founder and operations assistant to find answers and coordinate work.

**What I changed:** I reviewed the existing setup, connected the systems around the community platform and brought the team’s work into one place:

- **Checkout System:** Moved checkout to a platform with better sales features, ad tracking, payment-plan management and connections to the other systems.

- **Email Marketing:** Consolidated approximately 9,100 contacts from multiple legacy tools into a single, clean account. Rebuilt the tags and segments so customer activity could be tracked across the connected systems.

- **Team workspace:** Set up Notion for the team of seven, with documentation, tasks and workflows to help them manage delivery with less input from the founder.

- **Automated workflows:** Automated purchases, customer access, record updates and recurring team tasks, with monitoring and backups to keep the systems running reliably.

**The result:** The team can manage more of client delivery independently, with less manual administration and founder coordination.

## 2. AI tools & business knowledge

**Before:** The business’s frameworks and knowledge were spread across documents and platforms, making them difficult for the team and AI tools to use.

**What I changed:** With the core platform integrations completed and the business data unified, I built a foundation for trustworthy AI implementation:

- **Organised business knowledge:** Brought the business’s knowledge, databases and frameworks into Notion and structured them for use with AI tools.

- **Connected AI tools:** Connected the AI tools to the systems the team already uses, so information can move between them automatically.

- **Converting IP into AI Tools:** Built custom AI tools using the business’s own frameworks.

**The result:** The team can find business information and use its own frameworks through custom AI tools.

## 3. Tracking & reporting

**Before:** With multiple funnels, offers and payment channels, getting a trustworthy picture of business performance was difficult. Faulty tracking made Meta ad reporting unreliable. The founder couldn’t reliably see where customers came from or how advertising and funnels were performing.

**What I changed:** Rebuilt tracking across checkout, email marketing, website analytics and advertising so the business could follow customer activity and measure results:

- **Meta Ads & Pixel Overhaul:** Corrected the Meta tracking events and UTM setup to improve attribution.

- **Automated Audience Syncing:** Set up automatic syncing between email segments and Meta custom audiences, removing the need for manual exports.

- **End-to-End Funnel Visibility:** Connected the reporting so the business can track funnel conversions, launches, channel returns and customer spending.

- **Database Reconciliation:** Audited and reconciled 383 membership records, correcting previous false assumptions about customer churn and upgrades.

**The result:** The founder can now see which campaigns, funnels and offers generate revenue, with reliable tracking across advertising, checkout and customer purchases. That gives the business a clearer basis for deciding where to invest and what to improve.

## 4. Sales improvements & results

**Before:** The business had established offers and successful launches, but its checkout setup limited opportunities to increase revenue and measure pricing changes.

**What I changed:** I implemented order bumps and upsells, restructured checkout flows, revised pricing and used sales data to guide promotions.

### The Commercial Wins

- **46% higher average order value:** Increased through targeted order bumps and upsells.

- **$7,473 in additional revenue:** Generated through order bumps and upsells.

- **Consistent 3×–6× ROAS:** Rebuilt tracking and reliable reporting give the business confidence in its advertising performance and decisions about where to invest.

- **$51,764 more contracted launch revenue:** Delivered with fewer buyers through a revised pricing strategy.

## The result

The business now has connected platforms, less manual work and reporting it can trust. The team can manage more of the day-to-day work, and the founder has better information for decisions about sales and growth.

## Case Study — Swoop Baby

# Building the systems to support a growing service business

Swoop Baby had growing demand and an expanding team, but the founder was still coordinating enquiries, managing client information and keeping track of the team’s work.

I rebuilt the client journey, brought client tracking into one place and automated onboarding and follow-ups, so the team could manage more of the work independently.

---

## 1. Client Journey & Delivery Automation

**Before:** The founder was involved throughout the client journey, from enquiries and onboarding to follow-ups. As bookings grew, there was more work to coordinate.

**What I changed:** I rebuilt the client journey and automated recurring steps:

- **Enquiry & Intake Automation:** New enquiries are captured and assigned automatically.

- **Client onboarding:** Set up onboarding messages and scheduling to start automatically when a service is booked.

- **Follow-Up Sequences:** Set up automated follow-ups based on each client’s stage in the service.

**The result:** The team needs less input from the founder to keep client work moving.

---

## 2. Client information & team tasks

**Before:** Client details, progress updates and notes were spread across spreadsheets and team messages. Finding all client information required manual searching across different tools.

**What I changed:** I built a centralised database to give the team one place to manage client information and tasks:

- **Unified Client Tracking:** Brought each client’s details, progress and notes into one record.

- **Task & Delivery Management:** Set up task tracking so the team can see what’s done, what needs attention and who’s responsible for the next step.

**The result:** The team can see client information, progress and next steps in one place, so nothing slips through the cracks.

---

## 3. Bookings & team capacity

**Before:** Tracking bookings and team capacity was manual, making it difficult to know when more support would be needed.

**What I changed:** Set up tracking for enquiries, bookings and client delivery:

- **Conversion Metrics:** Set up tracking to show how many enquiries turn into bookings.

- **Capacity Forecasting:** Set up views showing current client work and how much capacity the team has.

**The result:** The founder can see demand and team capacity, helping them decide when to hire and how to share the work.

---

## The result

The business now has connected systems and less manual work, so the team can manage client delivery with less day-to-day input from the founder.
